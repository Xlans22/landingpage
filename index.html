01 // SAFETY SPECIFICATION

The Human-in-the-Loop Imperative

In alignment with Anthropic’s October 2026 frontier model safety benchmarks, our systems maintain a strict mathematical and operational separation between probabilistic inference and transactional execution.

01

Air-Gapped Advisory Inference

Foundation models operate strictly as read-only advisory engines. They generate structured candidate diffs, summaries, and schema extractions, but possess zero direct execution write-privileges to production databases, payment gateways, or enterprise transactional records.

02

Authoritative Human Gateways

Every proposed action requires manual review and authorization by an authorized domain operator. The interface presents side-by-side diffs, bounding-box citations, and confidence metrics—giving operators complete authority to review, modify, or reject recommendations.

03

Zero High-Risk Autonomous Action

We deliberately reject unconstrained autonomy in regulated sectors. Our systems do not issue autonomous clinical diagnostics, credit approvals, or legal determinations. Every output is grounded in verifiable enterprise documentation with source citations.
